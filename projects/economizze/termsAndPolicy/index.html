You can organize income, recurring commitments, installment purchases, and categories, track your financial outlook for the whole year, and receive reminders so you never miss a due date. 4. Shared Finance Spaces

Economizze allows you to create or join a shared finance space to manage a budget together with other people, such as family members. As the owner of a space, you can invite other users by email and assign them a role (owner, editor, or viewer) that determines what they can see and change. Financial data within a shared space (income, commitments, expenses, and categories) is visible to every member of that space according to their role. Inviting a member requires their email address, which is used solely to identify and grant access to the correct account.
